Knockout format

Win or go home.

Fixtures are decided by the organisers and the full schedule is announced before the event. Winning teams move on. Losing teams are out.

  1. Register

    Sign up your team by 13 December. Entry is ₹3,000 per team.

  2. Fixtures

    The organisers decide the match fixtures and announce the schedule before the event.

  3. Knockouts

    Regular matches run 20 minutes. Win and you advance. A draw goes straight to penalties.

  4. The final

    The last two teams play a 40-minute final for the title.

Draws & penalties

  • Every match must have a winner.
  • If a match ends in a draw, it goes directly to penalties.
  • 3 penalty kicks per team initially.
  • Sudden death if the scores remain level.

Basic etiquette

  • Respect the referee's decisions.
  • Respect opponents, teammates and organisers.
  • Maintain sportsmanship throughout the tournament.
  • No fighting or unnecessary arguments.
  • Avoid abusive language and unsporting behaviour.
  • Follow the match schedule and organisers' instructions.

Team responsibilities

  • Report at least 10 minutes before kick-off.
  • Only registered players are allowed.
  • Teams must use appropriate footwear for the turf.
  • Late arrival may result in a walkover.
  • Only team captains should raise official concerns with organisers.

General conditions

  • Referee decisions during matches are final.
  • Organisers reserve the right to resolve unforeseen situations.
  • Serious misconduct may result in disqualification.
  • All teams must agree to the tournament rules before participation.
